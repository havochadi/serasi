2 · Our approach

Three proven measures make up the fit score

Holland RIASEC50%

What it is · Psychologist John Holland’s model of career interests

Measures · Which of six types of work you enjoy most

Why · Used by MySkillsFuture; fit predicts performance (92 studies)

Big Five30%

What it is · The most-researched model of personality, plus honesty

Measures · Openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, stability

Why · Conscientiousness predicts performance in nearly every job

Work values20%

What it is · What a job must offer for you to feel satisfied and stay

Measures · Your top 3 of 8, e.g. stability, balance, room to learn

Why · Value fit predicts satisfaction and staying (172 studies)

The six RIASEC types · your top two become your profile
R · RealisticBuilderEnjoys hands-on work with toolse.g. technician, engineer
I · InvestigativeAnalystEnjoys solving hard problemse.g. data analyst, researcher
A · ArtisticCreatorEnjoys designing and creatinge.g. UX designer, video editor
S · SocialCarerEnjoys helping and teachinge.g. nurse, teacher
E · EnterprisingPersuaderEnjoys leading and persuadinge.g. sales, marketing
C · ConventionalOrganiserEnjoys order and clear systemse.g. accounts, operations
