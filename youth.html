About this pilot demo

Proposed pilot: 300 youths · Draft budget: S$22,000 · Preparation November 2026–March 2027 · Pilot April–June 2027 · Follow-up and decision by June 2028.

Partnerships, funding, job tasters and internships are proposals. No partner has been approached. Scores and role data are illustrative and have not been validated.

This demo saves quiz answers on your device. Ask Serasi sends your questions and a summary of your results to Google’s Gemini AI. The MENDAKI staff page uses made-up data.

Fees, subsidies, pay figures and world job-market notes were checked in October 2026 and change often. Confirm them with the official links.
